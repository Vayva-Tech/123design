## Page Purpose

The process page answers: "How does this firm actually take a product from concept to production?" It demonstrates structured thinking, technical depth, and a repeatable framework — without implying every project followed every stage.

---

## Lifecycle Stages

### CON (Concept)

**Purpose:** Define the product, validate feasibility, establish direction.

**Questions answered:**

- What are we building?
- For whom?
- Is it feasible?
- What are the requirements?

**Typical outputs:**

- Requirements document
- Product definition
- Architecture direction
- Industrial design direction
- Feasibility assessment
- Initial risk analysis

**Related capabilities:** Product Development, Industrial Design

**What NOT to claim:**

- Don't imply every project had a formal CON phase
- Don't claim specific methodologies not verified

---

### EVT (Engineering Validation Test)

**Purpose:** Validate core architecture and functional feasibility.

**Questions answered:**

- Does the core architecture work?
- Are key risks retired?
- Do ME/EE integrate?

**Typical outputs:**

- Functional prototypes
- ME/EE integration validation
- Architecture validation
- Risk retirement evidence
- Initial BOM

**Related capabilities:** Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Prototyping

**What NOT to claim:**

- Don't claim every project reached EVT
- Don't claim specific test equipment not verified

---

### DVT (Design Validation Test)

**Purpose:** Detailed engineering, verification, materials, tolerances, reliability.

**Questions answered:**

- Does the detailed design meet requirements?
- Are materials and tolerances correct?
- Is it reliable?

**Typical outputs:**

- Detailed engineering drawings
- Verification test results
- Materials validation
- Tolerance analysis
- Electronics revisions
- Reliability data
- Certification preparation

**Related capabilities:** Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Testing & Validation

**What NOT to claim:**

- Don't claim specific certifications (UL, FDA, FCC, CE, ISO) without owner verification

---

### PVT (Production Validation Test)

**Purpose:** Validate manufacturing process, tooling, assembly, quality.

**Questions answered:**

- Can this be manufactured consistently?
- Does tooling work?
- Is assembly process defined?
- Is quality plan in place?

**Typical outputs:**

- Tooling validation
- Pilot build results
- Fixture verification
- Assembly process documentation
- Quality planning
- Manufacturing validation report

**Related capabilities:** Tooling, Manufacturing, Testing & Validation

**What NOT to claim:**

- Don't claim specific factory relationships or geography without verification

---

### Production

**Purpose:** Ongoing manufacturing, quality control, continuous improvement.

**Questions answered:**

- Is production stable?
- Is quality consistent?
- Can we improve?

**Typical outputs:**

- Supplier coordination
- Manufacturing output
- QC data
- Assembly records
- Packaging
- Continuous improvement records

**Related capabilities:** Manufacturing, Tooling, Program Management

**What NOT to claim:**

- Don't claim production volumes, specific factory ownership, or geographic specifics without verification
